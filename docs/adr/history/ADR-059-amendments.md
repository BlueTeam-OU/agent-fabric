# ADR-059 — amendments

The full notes; the ADR's body reads current and its Amendments table lists them.

### Amendment 2026-10-16 — The OpenAI agreement is unverified

The record as merged in #278 said the OpenAI business account "has a
processing agreement", and excluded it from the record for that reason.
The opinion it rests on lists OpenAI's business terms and processing
addendum as unverified, and the redraft leaves a fact for the owner to
confirm that the agreement is accepted. The round-3 review of #278
found the record stating as fact what its source could not verify. The
Scope and §2 now say the agreement is offered and unverified, for the
owner to confirm. No decision changes: the Max plan and rules 1-4 are as
ratified; a rule binding client data to the OpenAI account would be a
decision of the owner's, not an amendment.
