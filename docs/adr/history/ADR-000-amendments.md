# ADR-000 — amendments

The full notes; the ADR's body reads current and its Amendments table lists them.

### Amendment 2026-09-27 — The coordinator's proposals adopted

The owner, 2026-09-27, to the fabric-coordinator session: the points
marked as coordinator proposals "are acquired in the corpus", and the
marker can be removed.

What changed. ADR-000 as ratified marked the coordinator's additions to
the owner's vision so that each could be struck alone: the pillar P7
(sustainable operation), five paragraphs (enduring is not unchanging;
verification over assertion; an explicit mandate, not implicit
supervision; failure is expected and survivable; federation needs
portable trust) and four sentences at the end of the P1, P2, P3 and P5
directions. The owner adopted all of them. The markers are removed; the
five paragraphs keep their titles as bold lead-ins; §7 no longer says
the proposals stand or fall individually; §8 and the Decision Makers line
record that they were drafted by the coordinator and adopted by the
owner. No rule of §5 changed its meaning.
