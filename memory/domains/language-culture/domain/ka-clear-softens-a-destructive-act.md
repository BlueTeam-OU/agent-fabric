## Georgian "გასუფთავება" (clear) reads as tidying a field or cache; on a button for a consequential act it softens it — name the act ("delete")

English "Clear" goes into Georgian as "გასუფთავება", right by the form
and wrong by the meaning: it says tidying, cleaning a field or a cache,
and it softens an act that destroys or stops something (gzapp: removing
an access key, which stops every image and video). A shared button
(`admin.common.clear`) therefore does not fit every dialog: where the act
has consequences the Georgian names the act ("წაშლა", delete) and the
dialog needs a confirm key of its own — a key-shape question for the
surface's owner. A second, independent reader (flutter-dev) cited the same
word as the example of "right by the shape, wrong by what it means".
