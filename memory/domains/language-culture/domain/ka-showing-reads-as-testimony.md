## "showing" as the noun ჩვენება reads as testimony beside "was interrupted"; say it with a verb or participle

The English "showing" (putting a message on screen) as the Georgian noun „ჩვენება“ first reads as a witness's or defendant's testimony, especially with „შეწყდა“ ("was interrupted"). A participle („ნაჩვენები იყო“) or a verb („ბოლომდე ვერ გამოჩნდა“, "could not be shown to the end") is unambiguous. Found at the first pass, 2026-10-24, j122, gzcoord's `delivery.may-repeat`, commit d5694a87 (branch for/python-dev-02/j89-carrier-keys-ge). On the same line, a literal "may repeat" read as a weaker `delivery.retransmission` ("act on it once"); it reads "possibly already seen", from the sender's gloss. Render showing with a verb or participle, and check a shared stem with a neighbouring key so one does not read as a variant of the other.
