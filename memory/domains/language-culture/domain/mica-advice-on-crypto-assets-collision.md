## on a consultancy page, write 'advice on crypto-asset technology', never 'advice on crypto-assets': MiCA names the latter as a crypto-asset service

MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114) lists "providing advice on crypto-assets" among the crypto-asset services (Article 3(1)(16), point (h), as I recall it; not checked against the Official Journal on 2026-11-11). A page saying a technical consultancy gives "advice on crypto-assets" can therefore read as a claim to a licensed service. The blueteam.ee disclaimer body says "advise you on the technology of tokens and other crypto-assets". I kept the meta description to "crypto-asset technology" (REPLY 01a1cbda-4012, j281, 2026-11-11).

**Why:** in legal copy, a plain-English phrase can be a defined term of the regulation.
**How to apply:** before shortening a phrase about crypto-assets, check it against MiCA's defined terms. If they collide, keep the word "technology". If the wording is in doubt, send it to legal-expert-ee-eu. Related: [[legal-condition-sentence-stays-whole]].
