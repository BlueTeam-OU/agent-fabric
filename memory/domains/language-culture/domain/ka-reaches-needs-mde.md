## Georgian "აღწევს" with a dative means attaining a goal; "reaches someone" needs -მდე

"X პირებს აღწევს" reads as "attains" on the model of "attains a goal". For "reaches people" write "პირებამდე აღწევს" or "პირებზე ვრცელდება". Found on j75, 2026-10-16, legal-expert ge charter lines 82-83 (f184097e).
