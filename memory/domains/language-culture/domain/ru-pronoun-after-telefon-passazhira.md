## After «Телефон пассажира…», Russian «его» reads as the phone; repeat the noun

In a two-sentence Russian string whose first subject is «Телефон пассажира…» (phone and passenger are both masculine), «его» in the next sentence reads at a glance as the phone. Repeat the noun («поищите пассажира»). Found by the cold read on the driver's no-sign bands (2df3bac43, gzapp#1119, 2026-10-15).
