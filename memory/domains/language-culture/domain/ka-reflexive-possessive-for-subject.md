## possession by the clause subject is the reflexive tavisi, not misi; English its becomes either

In Georgian, possession by the clause's own subject is expressed with the reflexive "თავისი", not "მისი". English "its" makes no such distinction, so "მისი" slips in mechanically: "the report shows its reader" must be "თავის მკითხველს". Found in j94, f0aa9af8. Check every "მისი" against the clause subject.
