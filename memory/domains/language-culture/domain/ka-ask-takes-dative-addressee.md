## "ask" (ჰკითხე) takes its addressee in the dative; a nominative turns the agent into what is asked

j121: my draft had "ის აგენტი ... ჰკითხე" (nominative) for "ask that agent"; the addressee of ჰკითხე is dative, "იმ აგენტს". The worker caught it at pass 1. In the same paragraph a relative "რომელშიც" bound to the nearest `REQUEST` instead of the REPLY; it was fixed with a separate clause. English "ask X" hides the case. Check the dative on every addressee-taking verb.
