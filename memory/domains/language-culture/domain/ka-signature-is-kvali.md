## signature (a betraying mark) — ხელმოწერა is a calque; the ge charter uses კვალი

English "signature" in the sense of a mark that betrays something is not rendered "ხელმოწერა" (to a Georgian reader that is only an autograph, which they then look for in the text). The ge charter uses "კვალი" (trace): line 83, brief line 58, and the heading at line 133 (j98, de6925bf); inside a sentence it is paraphrased, e.g. line 97 (j94, f0aa9af8). "Not taken on trust" = "სიტყვაზე არ გიჯერებენ" (a fixed idiom); "ნდობით მიიღება" is an artificial passive.
