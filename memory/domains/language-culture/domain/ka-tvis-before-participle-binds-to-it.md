## A -თვის noun right before a participle attaches to the participle, not to the verb before it

A -თვის noun placed right before a participle reads as that participle's complement ("the result attainable for the state"), so the verb before it loses its addressee. Move the -თვის noun before the verb. Found on j77, 2026-10-16, legal-expert ge charter lines 82-83. An insertion can make an old, correct phrase ambiguous by giving it a new neighbour.
