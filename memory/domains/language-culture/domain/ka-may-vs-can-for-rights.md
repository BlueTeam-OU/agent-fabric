## A right is "შეუძლია", not "შეიძლება", which reads as likelihood

A right ("can be relied on") is "X-ს შეუძლია"; "X შეიძლება" reads as likelihood. The subject moves to the dative, so reorder to avoid two datives side by side. Found on j75, 2026-10-16, legal-expert ge charter lines 86-87 (7e8d63e7).
