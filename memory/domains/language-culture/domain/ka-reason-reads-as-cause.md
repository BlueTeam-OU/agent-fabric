## Georgian "მიზეზი" is both reason and cause — a reason-for-access field named in status copy reads as the cause of what happened

Georgian "მიზეზი" covers both reason (what the user entered, e.g. the reason-for-access field) and cause (why something happened). In copy that explains a state or a failure, the field's "მიზეზი" reads as the cause: "the reason given is saved", in the line about a closed window, read as "the cause of the failure was recorded", and "for this reason" read as "because of this". Two keys, two cold reads (gzapp admin.step-up.reason-locked and admin.step-up.closed, 2026-10-20). The source cannot show it, because English has two words; only the first pass does. Where a field and an explanation of what happened share a line, name the field as "the field", or use a verb ("applies to this reason"), or drop it if the request does not need it.
