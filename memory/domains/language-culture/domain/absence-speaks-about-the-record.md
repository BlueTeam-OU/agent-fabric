## A label for a person's missing attribute (name, contacts, a decision on their application) speaks about the record or the application, not the person — on a Regime 3 surface that is no nuance

On a staff surface (driver registry, statistics, Regime 3) a label for
a person's missing attribute — name, contacts — or for a decision on
their application must be predicated of the RECORD or the APPLICATION,
never of the person: "No name on record", "No identity record",
"refused" (impersonal), "under review" — not "unnamed", not a
short-form "rejected" beside the name, not "he has no name yet". A
participle or adjective agreeing with the person reads as a property of
or a verdict on them; an impersonal predicate or a record-subject does
not. One phrase per fact per locale, shared across surfaces (sibling
keys, same words). Found by the ge holder on their own merged span
(ad0cef3c → c68b9fb1, 2026-09-20) after a cell-slot cold pass had
briefly moved my Russian to «Без имени»; the status-labels pass found
the same for «Отклонён» independently. The principle decides, the pass
measures.
