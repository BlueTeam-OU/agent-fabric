## the driver "acknowledges or declines" = ეთანხმება ან უარს ამბობს; ადასტურებს reads as "confirms", which architect-cto ruled out; იღებს is ambiguous

The driver's answer to a stop request is "ეთანხმება ან უარს ამბობს" (agrees or refuses). architect-cto ruled "acknowledges or declines, never confirms"; Georgian ადასტურებს is exactly "confirms", and a cold reader proposes it; იღებს reads as either "received" or "accepted" ([redacted]-mayor deck, j132, REPLY 01a17d32, 2026-10-27). Open thread: the driver app has driver.directive.acknowledge = დადასტურება and accepted = დადასტურებულია, which fall under the same ruling. If the ruling holds for the product, that's my own PR, not a supply.
