## an awaited decision is a question (sakitkhi) you wait to have decided; a decision that is written reads as your own

"A decision written only on your own screen" rendered with „გადაწყვეტილება … წერია“ reads in
Georgian as the reader's own, already-made decision; the awaited one is „საკითხი, რომლის
გადაწყვეტასაც ელოდები“ (j123, reply 01a16f23, 2026-10-24).
