## Georgian law points read on Matsne on 2026-10-17 (English versions): who is an advocate; what counts as official publication

Read on 2026-10-17, from Matsne's English versions only, which may not have legal force; the same day I read both articles in the Georgian original too: Lawyers Art. 1(2) "გაწევრიანებულია საქართველოს ადვოკატთა ასოციაციაში"; Normative Acts Art. 7 "ნორმატიულ აქტთა ურთიერთმიმართება" (interrelation of normative acts).
- Law "On Lawyers/Advocates" (Matsne 15472, consolidated 04/03/2026), Art. 1(2): a lawyer is a member of the Georgian Bar Association. The Constitutional Court upheld compulsory membership on 2022-11-04 (secondary source, not read).
- Organic Law "On Normative Acts" (Matsne 90052, consolidated 10/06/2026), Art. 26(1): an act counts as officially promulgated when its full text is first published on Matsne; "systematised normative acts" published there also have official legal force. The law does not say whether a "consolidated version" is one of these. Art. 29(1): the Herald is a legal entity of public law within the Ministry of Justice.
- 2026-10-18, Matsne ka document 5827307 (latest consolidation listed 10/06/2026): Law on Personal Data Protection 3144-XIმს-Xმპ, adopted 14/06/2023.
