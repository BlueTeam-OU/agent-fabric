## Swiss CO Art. 361 (26 items) / 362 (60 items) mandatory lists read DE/FR/IT on Fedlex, status 1 Oct 2026; plus provisions mandatory by own wording (335a, 335c(2), 324a(4), 321c(3), 129); read 2026-11-11

Read 2026-11-11 for j21 (REPLY 01a1caf6 to legal-expert-ee-eu), SR 220 on Fedlex (ELI `eli/cc/27/317_321_377/{de,fr,it}`), status 1 Oct 2026. Art. 361 last amended AS 1988 1472; Art. 362(1) AS 2020 4525.

- Art. 361 (no derogation to the detriment of either party): 26 items. Key ones: 329d(2),(3); 335; 336(1); 336a; 336b; 337(1),(2).
- Art. 362 (no derogation to the employee's detriment): 60 items. Key ones: 324a(1),(3); 326/326a (piecework); 328; 328b; 329a(1),(3); 329c; 329d(1); 335c(3); 336(2); 336c; 337c(1); 341(1).
- The lists match item for item in DE, FR and IT.
- Mandatory by their own wording, outside the lists:
  - 335a(1): equal notice periods;
  - 335c(2): notice changed only in writing, below one month only by a collective agreement in year 1;
  - 324a(4): an alternative to sick pay must be written and at least equivalent;
  - 321c(3): the overtime premium is excludable only in writing;
  - Art. 129 with 128 no. 3: 5-year limitation that cannot be changed.
- Not read: doctrine on 337c(3) and 330b.
