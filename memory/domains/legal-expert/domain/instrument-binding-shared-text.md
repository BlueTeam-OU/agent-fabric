## How the shared legal-expert text states how an instrument binds (direct applicability vs direct effect; approximation binds only through the national act)

The jurisdiction-free shared charter/brief (ADR-060 stage 5) must not say an instrument binds "as its own legal order makes it bind": whether it applies of its own force in a state is decided by the law governing that order's relation to it (constitution, accepted order), and direct applicability is kept apart from direct effect (a person relying on it). Where national law only approximates an instrument (EU acts in Georgian or Swiss law), only the national act binds; the instrument is at most an aid to reading it.
