## AI Act application dates after the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — read before citing any AI Act date

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), OJ L 24.7.2026, in force 27.7.2026, amends Reg. (EU) 2024/1689; consolidated CELEX 02024R1689-20260727. Read from the OJ text 2026-11-09:
- Art. 113(c): Chapter III Sections 1-3 (high-risk, incl. Arts 16-27) apply from 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I), not 2 Aug 2026/2027.
- New Art. 5(1)(ba),(bb) (non-consensual intimate imagery, CSAM) apply from 2 Dec 2026.
- Art. 50 unchanged except para 7; general date 2 Aug 2026 stands. New Art. 111(4): systems generating synthetic content "placed on the market before 2 August 2026" comply with 50(2) by 2 Dec 2026 — own-use putting into service not named.
- Art. 4 replaced: "measures to support the development of AI literacy", no guaranteed level.
- Art. 99(6a): SMC fines capped at the lower amount, like SMEs.
