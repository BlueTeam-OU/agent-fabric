## in a lock-screen notification put the action before the reason: the text is cut at two lines

In a notification that asks the rider to act, put the action before the reason even if the source ends with it: a lock screen usually cuts the body at two lines. Name the subject explicitly afterwards. Source: j107, gzapp 91d3d0cb7.
