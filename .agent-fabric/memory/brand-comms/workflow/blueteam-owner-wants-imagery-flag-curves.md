## What the owner rejects and wants in blueteam.ee's look — never a text-only page; the flag's blue/black/white sections with curves; woven imagery

On 2026-10-15 the owner rejected the "Ledger" redesign of blueteam.ee (grey ground, text only, a monospace work trail as the only visual): "far worse than before… no images, no schema, nothing catching the eye". What they kept from the former site and want: sections in the Estonian flag's blue, black and white, separated by curves (never straight edges); strong imagery on every page. They chose the "Woven, light" direction (blueteam.brand decision 0003): white hero with a woven-ribbon image on the right, flag sections with curves, picture cards, one generated image family (woven threads in blue/black/white, hands but no faces).

**Why:** the owner judges the site as a visitor; restraint that removes imagery reads as unfinished, and the flag rhythm is part of BlueTeam's identity.

**How to apply:** for any BlueTeam surface, start from imagery and the flag's section rhythm; show directions with real images on a canvas before building; the owner then iterates fast on small details (line breaks, colours, alignment) — render and measure each.
