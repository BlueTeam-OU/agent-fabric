## Owner's 2026-10-15 rulings on the new BlueTeam logo (woven star, Outfit wordmark, one blue) and how the mark was made

The owner chose, 2026-10-15: the star without the BLUE TEAM lettering, drawn as satin ribbons woven over and under in blue, black and white, "rounded tips" candidate A (gpt-image-1, transparent background; prompt kept beside s1.png in the session scratchpad and to be registered). They rejected flat geometric pentagrams and the softer "cloth" variants ("A was the best one"). Wordmark: "BlueTeam" in Outfit SemiBold (OFL), "Blue" #0072ce, "Team" black; the owner asked that the star's blue and the name's blue be the same, so the logo uses #0072ce on white and on black (not the lighter on-ink blue); on the blue section the name is white/black.

The vector mark is a potrace trace of A by colour layer (blue, black, white; white ribbons get a #c9d3de edge on white). Because it derives from a generated image, flag copyright/trademark registrability to the owner (lawyer).
