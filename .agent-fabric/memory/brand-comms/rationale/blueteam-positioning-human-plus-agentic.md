## Owner ruling on how blueteam.ee characterises BlueTeam OÜ — human consultants plus agentic efficiency; no team page yet

Owner ruling, 2026-10-15, on the blueteam.ee content renewal (job j20):

- The company is characterised as **human consultants (the same pattern as the old site: a consulting firm of experienced professionals, an audience of CTO/COO, a partner network) plus agentic efficiency**. It is not presented as a pure "agentic engineering" shop where the agents are the product.
- **No team page, and no AI personas on the public site, until there is more than one human to show beside them.**

**Why:** the owner wants clients to meet consultants first; the agent fabric is how they deliver faster, not who they are. A team page with one person beside 25 AI agents would misstate the firm.

**How to apply:** lead with people and their judgement, then the fabric as the method. Do not reopen the team or persona question until the owner says a second human has joined. Related: [[avatar-style-guide]].
