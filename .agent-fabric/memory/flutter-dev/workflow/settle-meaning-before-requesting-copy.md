## Before a copy REQUEST goes to three locale holders, settle with the meaning owner what each line tells the reader AND what the reader should do — otherwise every cold read reopens the meaning and each round costs three re-requests.

On the call-sign keys (gzapp, October 2026) the copy went round four
times, three locales each, because the meaning was not settled first:
the rider's hint said what the sign was but not what to DO on hearing
it; the signless driver band stated a fact with no action; its first
action ("look for them aboard") was impossible from the wheel and was
corrected to "ask who is getting off". Each gap was found by a locale
holder's cold read, rightly handed back as meaning, ruled by
architect-cto, then re-requested from all three holders. Earlier, the
plan hint's "live" held two locales for days because nobody had said
which of two senses it meant.

**How to apply:** for each key in a copy REQUEST, write down before
sending: the reader and their situation (where they are, what they can
do — at the wheel, standing at a stop); the fact the line states; the
one action expected of them, or "no action" explicitly; and any word
whose sense is ambiguous, defined from the contract. If any of these is
not in a decision record, ask the meaning owner (architect-cto) first,
then send one REQUEST. When a cold read comes back with a meaning
question anyway, collect all of them before re-requesting, so the
holders get one revision, not several.
