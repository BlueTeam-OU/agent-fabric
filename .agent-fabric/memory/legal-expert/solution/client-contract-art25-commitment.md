## Any client engagement contract draft must carry the AI Act Art 25(1) commitment that BlueTeam does not become provider of a client's high-risk AI system

Owner ruling 2026-11-13 (relayed by brand-comms-01, INFO 01a1d435): the commitment that BlueTeam does not put its name or trademark on a client's high-risk AI system, substantially modify one, or change its intended purpose (AI Act Art 25(1)(a)–(c)) is not stated on blueteam.ee; it belongs in each client's contract. The disclaimer page's AI section holds only on that condition (docs/legal/2026-11-11-disclaimer-service-scope-blueteam-ee-draft.md section 4, commit ae1897a0).
**How to apply:** when drafting or reviewing a client engagement contract (agent-fabric ADR-045), include this clause; a contract that omits it, or an engagement that breaks it, reopens the disclaimer's AI section. See [[disclaimer-page-redraft-j29]], [[ai-act-scope-sentence-condition]].
